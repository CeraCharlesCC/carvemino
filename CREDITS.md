# Credits

## Music

- Menu music: "Amur Waves" (1909), composed by Max Kyuss (1874–1942).
- Game music: "Kalinka" (1860), written and composed by Ivan Larionov (1830–1889).

The original compositions are in the public domain. Carvemino uses its own in-game arrangements and synthesized audio; no third-party recordings are included.
