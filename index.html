HOW TO PLAY

1. HOW TO PLAY

Choose a piece, choose a cell, reshape the piece, then drop it.

  1. 01
    CHOOSE A PIECE SELECT LBRB

    Switch between the falling pieces. The selected piece appears larger in FOCUS.

  2. 02
    CHOOSE A CELL D-PAD D-PAD / STICK

    Move the cursor in FOCUS. Its color shows whether the cell can be changed.

  3. 03
    RESHAPE THE PIECE A RIGHT FACE

    Remove a filled cell, or spend SCRAP to fill a dashed cell.

  4. 04
    DROP THE PIECE B BOTTOM FACE

    Drop the selected piece to the bottom. Fill a complete horizontal row to clear it.

→

HOW TO READ FOCUS

FOCUS shows the piece you are editing.

  • Filled cells can be removed with CUT.
  • Dashed cells can be added with FILL.
  • CUT shows edits left. SCRAP shows available material.
